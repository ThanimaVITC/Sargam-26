Sargam '26 · Guest

Anarkali Marakkar

Actor · Malayalam Cinema · Kochi

Born and raised in Kochi, Anarkali made her screen debut in 2016 as Darshana in Aanandam. The part reached her through cinematographer Anend C. Chandran, a friend of her sister's. She has worked in Malayalam cinema ever since, and is one of the most familiar faces of its new generation.

Uyare (2019), where she played Sariya D'Costa, drew the widest critical acclaim of her career so far; her recent work runs from the anthology B 32 Muthal 44 Vare to Mandakini and Gaganachari.

Thanima

Thanima is the official Malayalam Literary Association of VIT Chennai, dedicated to celebrating Kerala's language, art, and cultural heritage. With one of the largest regional student communities on campus, Thanima creates experiences that bring together students from diverse backgrounds through creativity, tradition, and entertainment.

Sargam is the flagship celebration of Onam at our institution, bringing together students, faculty, alumni, and guests in a vibrant tribute to Kerala’s rich cultural heritage. Rooted in the values of unity, gratitude, and togetherness that define Onam, Sargam offers an immersive experience through traditional art forms, captivating music and dance performances, pookalam competitions, cultural showcases, and festive activities.

Blending tradition with youthful energy, the event transforms the campus into a dynamic celebration of culture and community. More than a festival, Sargam is a platform that fosters connection, celebrates diversity, and creates lasting memories while preserving the essence of Kerala’s most cherished harvest festival. Sargam is more than a celebration: it is a convergence of culture, community, and collaboration.

Sargam '25

Sargam ’25, organized by Thanima at VIT Chennai, was graced by the esteemed presence of renowned actress Anna Ben as the Chief Guest. The event witnessed an overwhelming participation of over 1200+ students and faculty members, transforming the campus into a vibrant celebration of Kerala’s most cherished festival, Onam.
